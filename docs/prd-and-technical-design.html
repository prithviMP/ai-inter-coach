The hour worked if

A stranger finishes and points at a drill.

  • 1They reach the rubric without help.
  • 2The follow-up names something from their first answer.
  • 3At least one quote is a real span from the transcript.
  • 4They can say what the next drill asks them to practise.
  • 5The same name still finds the session after a refresh.

If the probe is generic, or the quotes are invented, fix the prompts before adding a feature.
